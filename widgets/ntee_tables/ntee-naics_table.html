NTEE Codes with NAICS Crosswalk

Every National Taxonomy of Exempt Entities (NTEE) code the IRS assigns, with its short and long description and the closest North American Industry Classification System (NAICS, 2022 edition) industry. This is the same table the NCCS data pipeline uses, so it matches the codes in the Business Master File. Codes the IRS has retired are kept because older files still carry them.
